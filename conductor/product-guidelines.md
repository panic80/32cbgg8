# Product Guidelines - CF Travel Bot

## 1. Prose Style
The CF Travel Bot employs a style that balances **Formal Authority** with **Helpful Support**:
*   **Structured Clarity:** Use official terminology and structured lists (bullet points, numbered steps) to present policy information.
*   **Professional Warmth:** While maintaining a formal tone, ensure the language is supportive and helpful, acknowledging the complexity of travel regulations.
*   **Directness:** Avoid fluff. Answers should be concise and directly address the user's query.

## 2. Brand Messaging and Values
Every interaction must reinforce these core values:
*   **Reliability:** Explicitly state that information is sourced from current official policy. "According to the CBI..." or "The NJC Directive specifies..."
*   **Support:** Position the bot as a partner in navigation. Use phrasing like "To help you understand your entitlements..." or "I've found the following information to assist with your claim..."

## 3. Visual Identity
The visual design should reflect a **Modern and Technical** AI assistant:
*   **Sleek Interface:** Use a modern UI aesthetic with clean lines and high-quality typography.
*   **Data Visualization:** Incorporate sleek visualizations for calculators and allowance breakdowns.
*   **Technical Sophistication:** A dark-mode friendly palette with subtle technical accents (e.g., code-like font for citations) to emphasize the AI-driven nature of the tool.

## 4. Communication Standards
*   **Clarity over Cleverness:** Avoid AI-specific jargon or overly complex sentences.
*   **Source Integrity:** Never present information without a traceable source.
*   **Mobile Optimizations:** Ensure all text and visual elements are highly legible on mobile screens.
