# Product Guide - CF Travel Bot

## 1. Initial Concept

The CF Travel Bot is a RAG-based assistant designed to simplify and clarify travel policies and procedures for the Canadian Armed Forces (CAF) and Department of National Defence (DND).

## 2. Target Audience

The primary users of this system include:

- **CAF Members and DND Employees:** Seeking clarity on travel policies and entitlement.
- **Part-time Class A Primary Reservists:** A key group requiring mobile-accessible guidance while transitioning between civilian and military roles.
- **Travel Administrators:** Managing official travel authorizations and processing claims.

## 3. Core Goals

- **Policy Clarity:** Provide instant, accurate, and cited answers to complex travel policy questions using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
- **Automated Assistance:** Simplify the calculation of meal allowances, incidentals, and kilometric rates based on current directives.
- **Process Streamlining:** Facilitate the creation and submission of travel claims by providing guided support.

## 4. Key Features

- **Conversational RAG Interface:** A chat-based assistant that links directly to official policy sources (CBI, NJC Travel Directive, etc.) for every answer.
- **Interactive Allowance Tools:** Built-in calculators for trip costs and allowances based on location, dates, and member status.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Tools for administrators to manage source documents, monitor system performance, and analyze usage metrics.

## 5. Design Principles

- **Authority and Trust:** Maintain a professional and authoritative tone consistent with official DND/CAF communication.
- **Accessibility and Mobility:** A clean, responsive UI that ensures usability on both desktop and mobile devices, catering to members in the field or on the move.
- **Transparency:** Always show the reasoning and the specific policy sections used to generate an answer to ensure accountability and verification.
